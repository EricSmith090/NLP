**M3 — adapter `LookupResult.readings` cannot emit slash-glued tokens.** (Status 2026-11-12: **UNADDRESSED — `dictionary/*` unchanged.** The variant fold added in `textdiff.py` does not split slash-glued tokens; it is a match-time fold, not a tokenizer change — the recall leak is unchanged.)
